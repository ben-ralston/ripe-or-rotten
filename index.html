How it works

Open the app and point the camera at your board. After a moment it locks onto the tiles and colors every word: green if it's in the dictionary, red if it isn't. You can also pick a photo with the Photos button.

A letter was read wrong

Tap the tile and pick the right letter. If something that isn't a tile was picked up, choose "Not a tile". Pinch to zoom on big boards.

Which dictionary does it use?

The NASPA Word List 2023, the North American tournament word list (about 197,000 words), licensed from NASPA. If your group prefers, switch to the free ENABLE list (about 173,000 words, without QI, ZA or OK) in Settings.

Still stuck?

Email compost.receipt_8f@icloud.com and say what happened. A photo of the board helps.

Privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026

Ripe or Rotten collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

Camera and photos

The camera feed and any photo you pick are read on your iPhone to find the letters. They are never saved by the app, never uploaded and never shared. The camera only runs while the scan screen is open.

What stays on your phone

Your dictionary setting is saved on your iPhone so the app remembers it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
